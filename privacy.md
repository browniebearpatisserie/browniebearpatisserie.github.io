Privacy Policy
Brownie Bear Content Manager
Last updated: October 20, 2026
Who we are. Brownie Bear Content Manager is a private, internal tool operated by Brownie Bear Patisserie, based in Indonesia.
Data we access. After the account owner authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app may access basic profile information, account statistics, the list of the owner's videos, and, where permitted, the ability to upload or publish videos.
How we use the data. Data is used only to show account statistics and to help the owner manage their own TikTok content. We do not use it for advertising or profiling.
Sharing. We do not sell, rent, or share TikTok data with third parties. Data may pass through service providers needed to operate the app, only for that purpose.
Storage. We do not keep TikTok data longer than needed to operate the app. Access tokens are stored securely by our integration provider.
Your choices. You can revoke the app's access at any time in TikTok settings, and you can ask us to delete any data we hold by emailing us.
